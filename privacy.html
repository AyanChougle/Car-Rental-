Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle your information with care.

Privacy Terms Refunds

1. Information We Collect

To provide our vehicle rental services, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Residential Address
  • Date of Birth

Identity Verification

  • Driving License
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport / PAN Card
  • Selfie or Photograph (if required for verification)

Booking Information

  • Vehicle selected
  • Rental duration
  • Pickup and return location
  • Booking history
  • Rental preferences

Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through trusted third-party payment gateways. We do not store your complete debit/credit card information on our servers.

Device & Technical Information

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • App Usage Statistics

Location Information

With your permission, we may collect GPS or location information to:

  • Verify pickup and return locations
  • Improve service availability
  • Protect our vehicles against misuse

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process bookings and payments
  • Verify identity and driving eligibility
  • Deliver and manage rental services
  • Send booking confirmations and reminders
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Improve website performance and customer experience
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations
  • Investigate accidents or disputes

3. Sharing of Information

We respect your privacy and never sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only with:

  • Payment gateway providers
  • Identity verification partners
  • Insurance companies
  • Roadside assistance providers
  • Government or law enforcement authorities where legally required
  • Service providers helping operate our platform

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted communication (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure servers
  • Access controls
  • Authentication systems
  • Regular security monitoring

While we take every reasonable precaution, no online system is completely secure. Therefore, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Improve website performance

You may disable cookies in your browser; however, certain features may not function properly.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Complete rental services
  • Meet legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Request a copy of your stored information

Requests can be made through our support team.

8. Third-Party Services

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Users should review their respective privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended only for users aged 21 years or above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions will be published on our website with the revised effective date.
