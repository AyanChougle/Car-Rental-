1. Eligibility

To rent a vehicle, you must:

3. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is collected before vehicle handover to cover potential incidentals.

Refundable deposit balances are initiated within 24–48 banking hours following vehicle return inspection, subject to:

The refunded amount is credited back directly to the customer's source account / UPI.

Rental duration begins at the scheduled pickup time and ends upon successful vehicle return.
