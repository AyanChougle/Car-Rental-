1. Eligibility

To rent and drive a vehicle with KRUIZLY, you must:

  • Be at least 21 years of age
  • Hold an original, valid Driving License
  • Provide valid Government-issued ID (Aadhaar, Passport)
  • Have a valid payment method in your name
  • International customers must possess an International Driving Permit (IDP) where applicable.

We reserve the right to reject any booking if verification criteria are not met.

2. Booking Confirmation

A booking is officially confirmed only upon:

  • Successful identity and document verification clearance
  • Receipt of advance / full payment
  • Vehicle availability schedule confirmation

KRUIZLY reserves the right to cancel bookings due to unforeseen operational or maintenance constraints with a full refund.

3. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is collected before vehicle handover to cover potential incidentals.

Refundable deposit balances are initiated within 24–48 banking hours following vehicle return inspection, subject to:

  • Vehicle condition & return inspection clearance
  • Zero outstanding traffic challans or municipal violations
  • Fuel level & FASTag toll reconciliation
  • Damage / incident assessment (if any)

The refunded amount is credited back directly to the customer's source account / UPI.

4. Rental Period

Rental duration begins at the scheduled pickup time and concludes upon successful vehicle check-in and handover.

Late returns may attract additional hourly charges as outlined below.

Failure to return the vehicle without prior approval may result in legal action and security forfeiture.

5. Vehicle Usage Rules

The renter agrees to drive responsibly, observe all traffic rules, and maintain vehicle care.

The vehicle must not be used for:

  • Racing, drifting, or speed trials
  • Towing or pushing other vehicles
  • Commercial passenger or goods transport without authorization
  • Carrying hazardous materials or contraband
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or narcotics
  • Extreme off-road terrain not meant for standard vehicular passage

Violation results in immediate contract termination, police notification, and forfeit of deposits.

6. Fuel Policy

Vehicles are delivered with a documented fuel level and must be returned with an equivalent level. Any shortage will be calculated based on actual fuel rate plus refueling service fee.
