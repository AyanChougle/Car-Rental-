Refund & Cancellation Policy

We make cancellations and refunds clear and easy for our customers.

Privacy Terms Refunds

1. Cancellation by Customer

More than 24 hours before pickup

  • Eligible for 100% refund.

Between 12 and 24 hours before pickup

  • A cancellation fee may apply.
  • Remaining balance will be refunded.

Less than 12 hours before pickup

  • Partial refund may be issued after deducting applicable cancellation charges.

After Rental Start Time

  • No refund will be provided once the rental period has begun.

2. Cancellation by KRUIZLY

If we cancel your booking due to:

  • Vehicle unavailability
  • Mechanical issues
  • Operational reasons
  • Force majeure

You will receive either:

  • A full refund, or
  • A replacement vehicle (subject to availability).

3. Security Deposit Refund

Security deposit refunds are initiated within 24–48 banking hours following vehicle return handover, after verification of:

  • Return inspection and vehicle condition assessment
  • Verification of returned fuel level
  • Clearance of e-challans, traffic fines, and tolls
  • Deduction reconciliation (if any damages occurred)

The refundable balance is transferred directly to the customer's source account / UPI.

4. Non-Refundable Charges

The following charges are non-refundable where applicable:

  • Late return fees
  • Extra kilometer charges
  • Fuel shortage charges
  • Cleaning charges
  • Damage repair costs
  • Traffic fines and tolls
  • Administrative processing fees

5. Refund Method

Approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method used during booking.

Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment gateway.

6. Force Majeure

Refunds may be affected by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to:

  • Natural disasters
  • Government restrictions
  • Strikes
  • Civil disturbances
  • Public emergencies
